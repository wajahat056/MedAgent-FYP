### ALT (Liver Enzyme) — HIGH

ALT stands for **alanine aminotransferase**, which is an enzyme found mainly in your liver. Think of it as a substance your liver uses to help carry out important chemical reactions. Normally, only very small amounts of ALT show up in your blood. However, when liver cells become damaged or stressed, they release ALT into the bloodstream, causing levels to rise.

The normal range for ALT is **7 to 56 U/L**, and your result came back at **689,578 U/L**. This is an extraordinarily and significantly elevated level — many thousands of times higher than the upper limit of normal.
